RAW-FABRID V1 representative sample

Source: Perez-Llorens, Albero-Albero and Silvestre-Blanes (2026)
DOI: 10.17632/db6g85xsyg.1
Licence: CC BY 4.0

The complete publisher archive exceeds 500 MB and is not included. This
authentic sample contains 15 original 256 x 256 grayscale patches and eight
matching defect masks. It covers all seven anonymised publisher origins,
both labels, and the train/validation/test assignments used by the study.

The sample is for testing the code and interface only. It is deliberately
small and is not suitable for reproducing the reported model metrics.
Download the complete V1 release with scripts/download_data.py before running
the full training or evaluation workflow. The exact sample rows and checksums
are recorded in data/sample-manifests/split.csv.
